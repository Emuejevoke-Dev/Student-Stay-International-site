Who We Are & Why We Are Doing This

Eradicating International Student
Accommodation Fraud Worldwide

Moving to a new country to study is an extraordinary life milestone. It shouldn't begin with rental scams, fake landlord wires, frozen deposits, or frozen radiator pipes. We created StudentStay to give every student radical transparency, 360° truth, and escrow safety.

Our Founding Story

We Were International Students Who Lost Money To Phantom Landlords.

In 2023, two international students arriving at Glasgow Airport from Ghana and India were told that the flat they paid a £1,200 deposit for didn't exist. The Facebook landlord vanished, leaving them stranded with heavy suitcases in the Scottish rain.

We swore no incoming student should ever experience that helplessness. We built StudentStay with a simple promise: zero scam risk. Every house is physically inspected by resident ambassadors, every room can be inspected in interactive 360°, and every deposit is locked in government-compliant escrow trust accounts until you hold the physical room keys.
