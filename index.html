Why International Students Trust StudentStay

Every year, international students lose millions to fake Facebook listings, ghost landlords, and bait-and-switch rooms. We eliminate fraud through 4 ironclad pillars:

Escrow Deposit Protection

Holding deposits are secured in regulated trust escrow. Money is NEVER paid directly to unknown landlords until keys are in your hand.

Interpersonal Verification

Every property and landlord identity is verified in person. Room measurements, locks, heating, and safety certificates are audited before listing.

Instant Clarification

No hidden costs or confusing tenancy small print. Direct legal and international student advisory support via WhatsApp within minutes.

Direct Peer Verification

Connect directly with current students who live in the building. Get raw, unfiltered, honest feedback before signing anything.
