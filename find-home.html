International Student Housing FAQs

Essential Insights Before You Book

Frequently asked questions answered by current international students and compliance advisors.

Are all utility bills (electricity, water, heating, and Wi-Fi) genuinely included?

Yes! 100% of our verified partner properties operate on an all-inclusive policy. This covers unlimited heating, hot water, electricity, and high-speed fiber internet (up to 250Mbps). You will never receive surprise winter utility surcharges.

How does the 100% "No Visa, No Pay" Escrow Guarantee work?

When you pay your holding deposit (£150), it is locked in a licensed legal escrow trust account. If your international student visa application is refused or delayed past course start date, simply forward your official Home Office notice and 100% of your deposit is refunded within 48 hours.

Do international students have to pay UK Council Tax?

No. Full-time enrolled students in the UK are completely exempt from Scottish and English Council Tax. Once you register with your university, you will download a Council Tax Exemption Certificate to submit to the local council.

Can I switch rooms or floors after booking?

Yes! From your Student Profile dashboard, you can request room reallocations, upgrades, or submit a tenancy transfer request if a friend or classmate takes over your contract.
